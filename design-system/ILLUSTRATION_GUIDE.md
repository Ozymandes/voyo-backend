# VOYO Illustration Guide

> Style guide for custom illustrations used in empty states, onboarding, and decorative contexts.

---

## Style Definition

**Kurzgesagt-inspired flat geometric illustration**

- **Shapes:** Simple geometric forms — circles, rectangles, triangles, rounded polygons
- **Depth:** Created through layering (overlapping shapes at different opacities), NOT shading or gradients
- **Perspective:** Flat front-view or isometric. Never 3D perspective.
- **Characters:** Minimal — circle head, oval body, dot eyes, no nose/mouth detail. Expressiveness through pose and eye shape.
- **Color:** Saturated, warm palette from brand colors. No pastel, no neon.
- **Line:** No outlines on shapes. Color fields define edges.
- **Texture:** Smooth. No grain, noise, or pattern fills.

## Required Illustrations

### Onboarding Series

**1. Adventurer (Step 1 — Travel Style)**
- Scene: A simple geometric figure (circle head, triangular body) standing at the base of a pyramid, looking up. Desert dunes in the background. Sun (large circle, desertGold) behind the pyramid.
- Colors: terra pyramid, desertSand ground, desertGold sun, sky figure
- Size: 200×200

**2. Cultural Montage (Step 2 — Interests)**
- Scene: Three vignettes arranged in a loose triangle: a mosque dome (religious), a plate of food (dining), an open book (history). Each vignette is a rounded rectangle with the scene inside.
- Colors: Category colors for each element
- Size: 200×200

**3. Nile Relaxation (Step 3 — Pace)**
- Scene: A felucca (sailboat) on a calm river. Simple wave shapes below, a triangular sail above. Small palm trees on the riverbank. Minimal, serene.
- Colors: skyBlue river, palmGreen trees, paper sail
- Size: 200×200

**4. Group Exploring (Step 4 — Companions)**
- Scene: 3-4 simple geometric figures walking together through an archway or gate. They're facing right (toward the future). An arch in warm stone color frames them.
- Colors: terra arch, sky/varying blues for figures
- Size: 200×200

### Empty States

**5. No Trips Yet**
- Scene: A figure holding a blank scroll/map. The map is empty. The figure looks curious but ready. Small question mark above the map.
- Colors: paper scroll, ink figure, caution question mark
- Size: 160×160

**6. No Recommendations**
- Scene: CleoOwl (simplified to geometric shapes) holding a magnifying glass, looking at a small pyramid. The owl is slightly puzzled (tilted head).
- Colors: sky owl body, vellum face, terra pyramid
- Size: 160×160

**7. No Search Results**
- Scene: A desert scene with small dunes and a tumbleweed (circle with lines). Wind lines suggest motion. Empty, but not sad — just quiet.
- Colors: desertSand dunes, smoke tumbleweed, stone wind lines
- Size: 160×160

### Error & Offline States

**8. Error State**
- Scene: CleoOwl with a confused expression (eyes are X shapes or spirals). A small crack or lightning bolt nearby suggests something went wrong.
- Colors: sky owl, caution lightning
- Size: 160×160

**9. Offline State**
- Scene: CleoOwl sitting calmly with eyes closed. A small airplane silhouette above (airplane mode reference). Peaceful, not alarming.
- Colors: sky owl, stone airplane
- Size: 160×160

### Decorative

**10. Splash Background**
- Scene: Wide panoramic scene of Egypt from left to right: Mediterranean coast (Alexandria lighthouse) → Nile Delta (green) → Cairo (citadel silhouette) → Giza (pyramids) → Luxor (temples) → Aswan (palm trees) → Red Sea coast → Sinai mountains. All as simple silhouette shapes along the bottom third of the image, with a gradient sky above.
- Colors: Single color (sky blue) silhouette on page background, or full region colors for a more vibrant variant
- Size: Full screen width, ~200px height band

## AI Generation Prompts

Use these prompts with ChatGPT, Midjourney, or DALL·E:

**General style prefix:** "Flat geometric illustration in Kurzgesagt style. Simple shapes, no outlines, no gradients, no shading. Warm saturated colors. Clean and modern. White background."

**Example for "No Trips Yet":**
> "Flat geometric illustration in Kurzgesagt style. Simple shapes, no outlines. A small person made of simple geometric shapes (circle head, triangle body) holding an empty scroll or map. They look curious and ready. A small question mark above the map. Warm cream background. Colors: warm brown figure, cream scroll, amber question mark. Minimal, clean, modern."

**Example for "Adventurer Onboarding":**
> "Flat geometric illustration in Kurzgesagt style. Simple shapes, no outlines. A person made of a circle head and triangular body standing at the base of a large pyramid made of a simple triangle. Desert dunes in background. A large golden circle sun behind the pyramid. No facial features on the person. Warm, adventurous, clean."
