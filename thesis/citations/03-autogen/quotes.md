### Accuracy flag
Prior lit-review cites "User Proxy / Assistant role split." That specific two-role split is described in the paper's "New Conversable Agent" examples (the math-problem and group-chat case studies). The verbatim lines above are the cleanest quotable definitions; the User-Proxy/Assistant naming is accurate but appears in worked examples rather than a one-sentence definition — quote it as "the Assistant agent and the User Proxy agent" only alongside a concrete example locator.
